# Commercial launch and IP review checklist

This is practical risk-reduction information, not legal advice or a guarantee that no claim can be filed. Consult a qualified attorney for your business and jurisdiction.

## Design precautions implemented

* Independent **Speedy Hosting** name and original wordmark dominate the site.
* The page follows broad web-design ideas rather than copying a specific Minecraft.net page pixel-for-pixel.
* No Mojang/Microsoft logo, proprietary Minecraft font, game UI graphic or texture is included.
* No publisher key art or official game screenshot is included.
* Illustrations are newly generated original environments; intentional recognizable proprietary characters are excluded.
* Game names are descriptive references to supported software, not Speedy branding.
* Prominent notices state: **NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT SERVICE. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT.**
* Third-party fonts, icons and renderer code have included open licenses.

## Still required before a real business launch

1. Have counsel review the overall resemblance, trade-dress and trademark context. Individual colors, square buttons or voxel art do not by themselves establish legal clearance.
2. Review each generated image for unintended resemblance to protected characters or published artwork. AI generation is not a non-infringement warranty.
3. Confirm the business/operator name, jurisdiction, real support contact and business address where legally required. The concept currently does not identify a verified operational hosting business.
4. Provide a working contact method, real terms, privacy policy, billing/refund policy and consumer disclosures. The included policy dialogs are prototype disclosures, not production legal documents.
5. Review current applicable game publisher terms, EULAs and server monetization rules. Do not assume that a fan-use allowance covers every commercial company or unrelated game service.
6. Verify the actual supported games, server requirements, prices, locations, advertised features and service availability.
7. Connect secure authentication, payment, billing, provisioning and support systems before taking orders.
8. Confirm rights to the hosting brand, domain, future photos, icons, additional fonts and any uploaded materials.
9. Do not add official game logos, promotional artwork or proprietary assets later unless their specific use is authorized.

## Primary Minecraft guidance consulted

* [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)
* [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/eula)
* [Microsoft Services Agreement](https://www.microsoft.com/servicesagreement)

The usage guidelines distinguish the Minecraft name, brand and assets; prohibit implying official endorsement; restrict commercial exploitation; permit descriptive secondary-name uses under conditions; and call for a prominent non-affiliation notice. They also state that permissions may change or be revoked and recommend consulting an attorney when uncertain.

The project makes reasonable design precautions. It does **not** claim that all possible rights, local laws or business obligations have been cleared.
