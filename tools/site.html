THE GOOD-TO-KNOW STUFF

Just a few things before you jump in.

How do I choose the right plan?

For a small vanilla Minecraft group, start with 4 GB. Choose 8 GB for bigger worlds and lighter modpacks, or 16 GB for demanding packs. Requirements vary by game, mods and player activity. Our configurator gives you an illustrative starting point.

Can I install mods and custom modpacks?

That’s the idea! The proposed Speedy service includes one-click modpack installs and full file access for custom setups. The panel above is an interactive demonstration, not a live server.

Can I bring my existing world?

The proposed workflow is simple: back up your world, stop the server, upload the world through the file manager and restart. Always keep your own backup before moving a world. The file manager here is a preview.

What if my community outgrows its plan?

The proposed service supports upgrades as your community grows. More RAM gives demanding worlds and modpacks extra headroom. You can compare example configurations now; actual migration and billing require a connected hosting backend.

Can I order a real server on this site?

Not yet. This is a front-end design concept. You can configure an example plan and download a quote, but no servers are provisioned, no payments are collected and no support messages are sent.
